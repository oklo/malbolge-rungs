## Targeted follow-up on the best base geometry

I then examined the first configuration directly:

- config index: `0`
- CRAZY positions: `(2, 5, 6, 9)`
- legal operand bytes: `(120, 88, 87, 84)`
- landings: `81, 88, 90, 114, 115, 162, 163, 169, 189, 190, 199, 249`
- splits: none
- offsets: default `()`

Under a targeted existence probe (`cap=1`, `attempt_budget=30000`) this
geometry had tails for 9 of the 12 lanes. The three dead inputs were:

- `0x90 -> 0xc1`
- `0x9c -> 0xcd`
- `0xf9 -> 0xa8`

That is materially stronger than “the whole config failed”: it isolates the
problem to three specific lanes in an otherwise live-looking geometry.

## Negative results

I tested three obvious ways to rescue those lanes in the same geometry.

1. Longer NOP runway before the tail body.
   - Tested `max_k = 8, 12, 16, 20`
   - Result: `0x90`, `0x9c`, and `0xf9` remained dead at every runway length

2. Deeper CRAZY chains in the existing tail catalog.
   - Extended the maximum CRAZY suffix depth from 4 up to 8
   - Result: the same three lanes remained dead

3. A broader short-tail grammar.
   - Brute-forced every tail body over `{MOVD, ROT, CRAZY}` of length up to 5
     (with the normal NOP prefix search still enabled)
   - Result: no such short tail exists for `0x90`, `0x9c`, or `0xf9` in this
     geometry

This last point is the strongest new result from the attempt: for the most
promising zero-split map12-hi geometry I found, two of the public heuristics
("more runway" and "more CRAZY") do not help, and even the larger local grammar
of short tail bodies still cannot realize the three hard lanes.

## Conclusion

I did not produce a candidate program for `L2.FM2h.xor51-map12-hi`.

The new evidence suggests the blocker is not simply search budget. In the
current two-stage dispatch family, the difficult part is the tail language
available after the second jump. A future attempt should change that language
qualitatively — for example with a different station/tail contract or a tail
family that allows a second nontrivial data redirection after CRAZY effects —
rather than only increasing the existing bounded search.
